\noindent Dear Editors,

\noindent Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled ``ForeSight: Time Series Forecasting with Temporal Embeddings and Weightless Neural Networks'', which we are submitting for exclusive consideration for publication in the special issue ``Advances in Open Source Forecasting Software'' of the International Journal of Forecasting.

Time series forecasting increasingly relies on open-source tools that support accessible, transparent, and reproducible workflows. This work presents ForeSight, a method that combines temporal embeddings with weightless neural networks, and integrates it into TSPredIT, an open-source R package for time series forecasting. We also develop an evaluation workflow within TSPredIT to structure the comparative assessment of forecasting methods, from data selection and preparation to model training, tuning, prediction, and result analysis.

The evaluation covered hundreds of time series across diverse domains and frequencies, and the results show that ForeSight achieved the best average rank among the evaluated methods while retaining the computational efficiency characteristic of weightless neural networks. Its integration into TSPredIT, together with the evaluation workflow developed within the package, supports reproducible and systematic comparisons of forecasting methods. We believe that the paper's methodological contribution, empirical results, and open-source software contributions align with the aims of the special issue.
